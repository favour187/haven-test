# Haven picture credits

The pictures in `assets/pictures/home_*.jpg` and `water.jpg`, `juice.jpg`,
`milk.jpg`, `rice.jpg`, and `bread.jpg` were generated for Haven. They are
illustrative starter pictures, not photographs of individual users.

The following starter pictures were resized and re-encoded from files on
Wikimedia Commons. The original, creator, licence and any changes are:

- `pictures/apple.jpg`: Evan-Amos, [Honeycrisp Apple](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Honeycrisp-Apple.jpg), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `pictures/banana.jpg`: robin_24, [Banana isolated on white](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Banana_isolated_on_white.jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/); resized and padded with white.
- `pictures/biscuit.jpg`: Paolo Neo, [Biscuit on white background](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Biscuit_on_white_background.jpg), public domain.
- `pictures/brushing.jpg`: Jonas Bergsten, [Toothbrush](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toothbrush_20050716_004.jpg), public domain.
- `pictures/washing.jpg`: Mark Buckawicki, [Hand washing man](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hand_washing_man.jpg), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The images are optional visual supports. People can substitute photos of their
own objects and choose symbols or text on their home screen.
