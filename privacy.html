Haven Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Haven is a private, offline-first daily-life companion. This policy explains what Haven does with your information. The short version: Haven keeps everything on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you actively choose a feature that needs the network.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Haven stays on your device:

  • Notes, journal entries, mood and body check-ins
  • AAC cards, schedules and "what will happen" plans
  • Sensory profiles, meltdown plans and scripts
  • Appointments, energy budgets and focus sessions
  • Settings and accessibility preferences
  • Any photo or audio you ask Haven to read

On Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux this is stored in a local database encrypted with SQLCipher. In a browser it is stored in that browser profile using local storage — still local, still never uploaded, but not encrypted at rest in the same way.

There is no account. Haven does not know who you are, and there is nothing to sign in to.

What is never collected

Haven has:

  • No advertising
  • No analytics — no crash reporting, no usage tracking, no telemetry
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No data broker or resale of any kind

When Haven uses the network

Haven works fully offline. The only features that use the network are ones you start yourself:

Buying Haven Plus

Checkout goes through Paystack. You type your email and Paystack handles the card or bank payment on its own page; Haven never sees your card details.

When you come back to Haven, it asks a small server (a Cloudflare Worker) to confirm with Paystack that the payment really went through. That check sends the purchase reference only — no notes, no personal content, no device data. Haven never trusts the browser to say a payment succeeded; only a server-side verification unlocks Plus.

Optional cloud extras

If you deliberately turn on an optional networked extra (for example a server-side summary), only what that feature needs leaves the device, and only when you press the button. Turning Offline only on in Settings stops every network call, including these.

Downloading an offline AI model

The offline AI models are optional downloads from a public model repository (Hugging Face). When you choose to download one, that request goes to the model host — Haven sends no information about you with it. Once downloaded, the model runs entirely on your device: prompts, images and audio never leave it.

Wearables (Bluetooth)

If you connect a wearable, Haven reads supported readings directly over Bluetooth LE from the device: heart rate, battery, blood pressure, pulse oximetry, and step/sleep records from a Bluetooth Physical Activity Monitor.

  • Readings are stored on your phone with the rest of your data.
  • Readings are never uploaded to Haven or to any server.
  • Vendor-specific features depend on what the wearable exposes; Haven does not

    • guess at data it does not receive.

  • Disconnect the wearable at any time in Settings.

On Android, Haven reads this directly over Bluetooth and does not require Health Connect or any other health-data app.

Camera, microphone and photos

Haven asks for these permissions only for features you start:

  • Camera / photos — to read a sign, label or letter on the device, or to

    • attach an image to a note.

  • Microphone — only to record audio you ask Haven to transcribe.

When the offline AI is installed, this processing happens on the device. It is not uploaded.

Trusted people

If you add a trusted person, Haven can open your phone or SMS app with a message ready to send. Haven never sends the call or the message itself — you review it and you press Send.

Children

Haven is designed for four age bands: 4–7, 8–12, 13–17 and 18+.

  • Child and middle bands (under 13) make no network calls at all, beyond

    • nothing — the networked features above are not available to them.

  • No data from a child's device is ever collected, because Haven collects

    • nothing in the first place.

Your controls

  • Export anything you have written.
  • Delete individual entries, or clear everything from Settings.
  • Turn on Offline only to cut every network connection.
  • Set a PIN to lock the app.

Deleting your data in Haven deletes it from the device. Because there is no server-side copy of your content, there is nothing else to erase.

Data security

The local database is encrypted with SQLCipher. The encryption key is derived and stored by the app on your device; Haven has no way to recover it, and therefore no way to read your data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the change will be described in the app's release notes and on this page. Haven will never start collecting data that this policy says it does not collect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the Haven repository, or email the developer at the address listed on the store listing.

A note on health information

Haven is a daily-life companion. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. It does not replace professional care or the support of a trusted person, and should not be used for urgent concerns.
